About

I think the best engineers are the ones who keep asking why until the answer feels boring — and then ask once more. That's mostly what I'm doing here.

Day-to-day, I work on the platform-security layer at Pegasystems — the unglamorous-but-essential plumbing of authentication, API gateways, and authorization. I love it because security is where small details compound into large consequences: a wrong default, a missed claim, a stale token, and weeks of cleanup. Getting it right means a system that almost no one notices, which is exactly the point.

The longer I do this, the more curious I get about the next layer: how the same care that makes auth systems trustworthy translates to AI systems. How do we know a model is doing what we think it's doing? What does "secure" even mean when the inputs and outputs are continuous? Lately I've been working through the math behind embeddings and retrieval — partly because RAG is everywhere now, mostly because I want to understand it from first principles. This site is where I write that thinking down.
